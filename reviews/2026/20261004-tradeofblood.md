Back in the world of Ana and Din and their mysteries, I'd been looking forward to this book. For me though, this is the weakest of the three, although reviews seem to disagree. After opening up into a very expansive mystery last time around and getting to 'see' more of the world, this was a small setting with a cast that led to what felt like quite a predictable outcome. I found myself speeding through the end partly because it was frustrating being able to see it all play out before the detectives could. 

That said, the commentary on the environment, farming, and agriculture was interesting at the back, and I can see what the author was trying to do with this one. Still very enjoyable, but didn't top the others.
