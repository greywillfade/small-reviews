This book grew on me in the last half, but overall I wanted to like it more than I did. It's a very clever book, one of detailed, non-hand-holding world building with a lot of local dialect terms and interesting progressive concepts for the time it was written, but the first half is basically setup. It has the Envoy ('alien' main character) in his role interacting with various natives of Winter, and for a long time I didn't really understand where the plot was going... until it all starts going wrong for the protagonist and we get more of a sense of direction and pacing. Ultimately it had quite a predictable ending, but also one that made sense within the plot. I can see why it's very highly regarded as a book, I just didn't love it sadly.
