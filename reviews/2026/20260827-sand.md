I'm really really glad that I dug this out, even if it did mean I had to completely unpack and re-tidy an entire bookshelf to find it (and of course it wasn't there, it was on _another_ bookshelf). Although I read them back to front, I actually really enjoyed it this way, and it kind of worked. 

You learn a lot about the whole family and their stories, plus Violet (a big character in 2) turns up for a little bit before the end. I had only really got some vague images of Palmer's Danvar excursion in my head and the rest of the plot felt exciting and new to read, even if I knew a lot about what ultimately happened because of book 2. I love the writing style and whilst for me these aren't _quite_ up there with the brilliantness of the Wool books, they're definitely close.
