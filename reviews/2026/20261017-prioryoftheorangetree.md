At 800+ pages long this was a CHONK of a book. It started off feeling like some of the chonkiness was probably unnecessary... seemingly every character we cone across, important or not, feels like they were described in detail to the level of 'Lady X, a [insert detailed description of thei looks], who comes from [insert description of that place], daughter of [detailed description of their parents' back story], took up the reigns of her [named horse, horse's origin story]'. This goes on for a while and whilst it does eventually begin to build up a detailed picture of the world, I found that after 200+ pages I was wishing it could get on with it a bit. 

At several points I thought this may have benefitted from being individual, shorter books, an opinion that I actually changed towards the end. It _does_ all hang together well in the end, I just think it could probably have done with some trimming, editing, and a bit more sophistication in parts. That said, I really enjoyed it by the end. I was fully invested in all the main characters, I loved the fact that this was a heavvy "girl power" read (plus token man), and I really enjoyed the world. I didn't enjoy the weirdly ambiguous and sudden ending at the end of the book for one of the main characters, where internet comments from the author say there's a reason for it (assume another book) but it just felt weird.

In the end I was tossing up whether to put this as a 3.5 or 4, and I've fallen on the lower side. It came down to whether this would be up there to recommend to people, and I don't know... probably yes, but because it's such a huge investment in time and trust to get to the good stuff, I've taken it down a bit. I'm going to move on to A Day of Fallen Night next though, so maybe I'll revise my reviews after some reflection.
