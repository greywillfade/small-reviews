I'd been patiently waiting for the paperback version to come out so I could buy it, but after getting into library life and they very kindly bought the hardback on my request I'm so pleased it didn't disappoint. It picks the story right up again after the first book, jumping straight into Amina and her crew's adventures to acquire the promised Transgressions. After the first section I mistakenly thought it could go down the road of "...and this quarter of the book is another short story about getting another one" but I was glad that wasn't the case. The majority is spent in one location, in a rather tricky situation. Unlike the last book where everyone's wellbeing felt quite safe, that wasn't the case this time and I couldn't tell how it was all going to pan out. 

The ending finished on a real cliffhanger, and it'll undoubtedly be a long wait to find out how everything pans out (ends?) but in the meantime I thought this was another great installment for those who like 40+ sweary pirate mums who love their kid but also value their identity and going on adventures (relatable).
