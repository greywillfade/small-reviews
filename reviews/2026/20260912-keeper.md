This is a very pretty, simple game, but ultimately one that I didn't really click with. It's yet another no-dialogue, visually cinematic game which is mostly 'push stick to move', interspersed with some minimally difficult puzzles. The start of the game was very tedious, and whilst it did get more interesting at the end, overall it felt like quite a slog. I do enjoy simple games and they definitely have their place, but for me whilst novel and pretty this just didn't feel delightful.

Another issue I had was that the story details and context seem to be in the achievement descriptions. It took me about a third of the game before I realised how to unlock the achievements, but even then to read the description you have to jump out of playing the game. As such I basically just ignored it, which again didn't help the game resonate.

What was unexpected though, is how much my young kid enjoyed playing a bit of this. I was basically at the end, and they helped drive around some of the (spoilers) boat section, and particularly enjoyed the wheel section. Seeing it through their eyes helped endear me to the game a tiny bit more, but overall a bit of a disappointment.
